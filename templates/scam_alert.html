Scam Alert

Stay safe from scams, fraud and suspicious messages.

⚠️ STOP BEFORE YOU ACT

If someone asks you for your password, PIN, bank details or money unexpectedly, stop and check before doing anything.

🚨 Common Scams

  • Bank scams: Someone pretends to be from your bank and asks for your PIN, password or OTP.
  • Phone scams: A stranger calls and asks for money or personal information.
  • WhatsApp scams: Someone sends a suspicious message or asks you to send money urgently.
  • Prize scams: You are told that you have won something but must pay money first.
  • Impersonation scams: Someone pretends to be a family member, friend, police officer or government official.

🔍 Warning Signs

  • Someone pressures you to act immediately.
  • Someone asks for your PIN or password.
  • You receive a message from an unknown number.
  • Someone asks you to send money unexpectedly.
  • You are promised money or a prize you never entered for.
  • Someone asks you to click a suspicious link.

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

  • Never share your PIN or password.
  • Never share an OTP with anyone.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Verify unexpected requests with the person directly.
  • Take your time. Scammers often create urgency.
  • If something feels suspicious, ask someone you trust for help.

📞 Think You Have Been Scammed?

Do not panic. Stop communicating with the person and contact your bank or another trusted organisation using an official telephone number.
