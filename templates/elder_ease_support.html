❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join an activity?

Go to Activities, choose an activity and select the join button.

How do I contact someone in my community?

Open the Community section and interact with posts or comments.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious message?

Open Scam Alert in Help & Safety for information about common scams and how to stay safe.

What should I do in an emergency?

Use the SOS feature or contact emergency services immediately.

🛡️ Need Immediate Help?

If you are in immediate danger, do not wait for app support. Contact emergency services or someone you trust.
